How to play

The big board is made of nine small tic-tac-toe boards. Win three small boards in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win the game.

  1. Pick a board. Highlighted boards are the ones you may play in. Tap one to zoom in.
  2. Place your mark in an empty cell. Tap another cell to change your mind, or go back to all boards.
  3. Confirm the move. The board zooms out.

Where you play decides where your opponent plays. The cell you pick sends them to the matching board: play the top-right cell and they must play in the top-right board.

Get three in a row in a small board to win it. A small board that fills up with no winner counts for nobody.

Classic rules: once a board is won or full it is closed. If you are sent to a closed board, you may play in any open board.

Open boards variant: a won board stays open while it has empty cells, and you must play there if sent to it. Moves there never change who won it. Only a full board gives a free choice.

The game ends in a draw when nobody can complete a line of boards any more.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter selects, Esc goes back to all boards.
