How to play

Take turns placing your mark in any empty cell of the 15×15 grid. Get five in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win.

  1. Tap where you want to play. On a small screen the board zooms in on that spot first.
  2. Place your mark in an empty cell. Tap another cell to move it.
  3. Confirm the move, or tap your mark again. Whole board goes back without moving.

Freestyle (the usual rule): five or more in a row wins.

Exactly five: only a line of exactly five wins. Six or more in a row doesn’t count, and the game goes on.

If the board fills up with no winner, the game is a draw. Players take turns to go first.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter places your mark and Enter again confirms it. Esc cancels.
